01 / DATA & PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Pravah International (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects business confidentiality and personal data privacy across all jurisdictions in which we operate, including the United Arab Emirates (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 — UAE PDPL), the Republic of India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), the United Kingdom, and the United States.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a 14-Day Pilot Request or scope enquiry on our website, we collect only the business contact details you voluntarily provide: Operator Name, Fleet/Company Name, Work Email, WhatsApp Number, Fleet Size, and Primary Operating Hub.

2. How Your Data Is Processed & Stored

Your enquiry is routed through a dual-channel dispatch system: (a) encrypted email delivery to Pravah International's executive desk for audit logging and proposal preparation, and (b) direct WhatsApp handover initiated by you. We never sell, rent, or broker client or fleet lead data to third-party marketers.

3. Live Sandbox Environment

The interactive WhatsApp simulator on our homepage executes locally in your browser using sample data. No real messages, passport copies, or financial authorisations are transmitted through the demo interface.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We store a single functional preference key (pravah-cookie-choice-v1) in your browser's localStorage to remember whether you selected “Accept” or “Essential Only”. Optional analytics remain disabled unless explicitly accepted.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

Enquiry records are retained only as long as necessary to evaluate, onboard, or support your deployment. You may request access, correction, or permanent deletion of your contact records at any time via our official WhatsApp executive desk (+91 91061 87102) or executive email.

02 / COMMERCIAL & SERVICE FRAMEWORK

Terms of Service

1. Scope of Engagement

Pravah International provides AI workflow engineering, 24/7 WhatsApp & Voice Concierge deployment, and bespoke web architecture for B2B operators. Indicative tier investments listed on the website serve as commercial benchmarks; final deliverables, timelines, and retainers are governed by the mutually approved Statement of Work (SOW) or Pilot Agreement.

2. 14-Day Fleet Pilot Terms

Approved operators receive a structured 14-day pilot deployment configured around their active fleet rates and operating hours. During the pilot, the operator retains full ownership of all inbound leads, customer conversations, and final booking approvals.

3. Operator Responsibilities & Human Oversight

The client operator remains responsible for accurate vehicle pricing, deposit policies, rental contracts, local licensing compliance, and final verification of renter identity documents prior to vehicle handover.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure

Live deployments integrate with enterprise platforms selected for the client's stack (such as WhatsApp Business API / Meta, Make.com, n8n, Supabase, ElevenLabs, and approved payment gateways). Service availability of third-party APIs is governed by each provider's respective SLA.

5. Intellectual Property

Upon full settlement of applicable setup fees, custom client prompts, fleet rate configurations, and bespoke web deliverables built specifically for the client are licensed for the client's exclusive business operations, while Pravah International retains rights to its underlying architectural frameworks.

03 / SECURITY & DATA PROTECTION

Security Architecture

  • Transport Encryption: All web traffic and API dispatches are enforced over TLS/HTTPS with strict Content-Security-Policy (CSP) and Referrer-Policy controls.
  • Zero Browser Secret Storage: Sensitive API keys, customer identity documents, and payment tokens are never stored in browser localStorage.
  • Direct Operator Handoff: In live fleet deployments, tourist Passport and International Driving Permit (IDP) submissions are routed directly to the fleet operator's designated secure WhatsApp or CRM environment with role-based access control.
  • Payment Isolation: Damage deposits and rental charges are processed strictly through the operator's approved payment gateway (e.g., Stripe, Checkout.com, Network International); Pravah International never stores raw cardholder data.
